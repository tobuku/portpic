About PortPic

A fast visual port reference for devices, cables, and adapters.

What is PortPic?

PortPic helps you identify the ports on your devices. Find a device by model, see high-resolution photos of every port and connector, and figure out which cable or adapter you need.

What you'll find

  • Device pages with port layouts, specs, and photos for switches, ONTs, routers, phones, and more.
  • Port guides covering USB-C, HDMI, DisplayPort, Ethernet, optical audio, 3.5mm, and docking stations.
  • Cable recommendations so you can buy the right cable the first time.

Built for speed

Every page is static HTML. No frameworks, no databases. Pages load fast on any connection.
