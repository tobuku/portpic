Dock and hub checklist

A dock adds ports. But only if your laptop supports the features.

Before you buy

  • Check if your USB-C port supports video output. Not all do.
  • Check if your USB-C port supports power delivery. Needed for charging through dock.
  • Check the watt rating. Dock pass-through must match your charger.

Hub vs dock

  • Hub. Portable. Usually bus-powered. Fewer ports.
  • Dock. Stationary. Has its own power supply. More ports.
  • Thunderbolt dock. Highest bandwidth. Most external displays supported.

Display limits

  • USB-C with DP Alt Mode. Usually one external display.
  • Thunderbolt 3. Up to two 4K displays.
  • Thunderbolt 4. Guaranteed two 4K or one 8K display.
  • DisplayLink docks use software drivers. Lower performance.
Recommended gear
USB-C hub with HDMI Thunderbolt 4 dock USB-C charger, 100W
