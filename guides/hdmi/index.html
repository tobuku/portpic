HDMI versions and limits

Not all HDMI ports are equal. Version determines what works.

Version basics

  • HDMI 1.4. Supports 4K at 30Hz. No HDR.
  • HDMI 2.0. Supports 4K at 60Hz. HDR support.
  • HDMI 2.1. Supports 4K at 120Hz, 8K at 60Hz. eARC, VRR.

ARC and eARC

  • ARC sends audio from TV to soundbar over HDMI. Limited formats.
  • eARC supports lossless audio. Requires HDMI 2.1 on both devices.
  • Check which HDMI port on the TV supports ARC. Usually only one.

Cable rules

  • High Speed cables handle up to HDMI 2.0 features.
  • Ultra High Speed cables needed for HDMI 2.1 features.
  • Cable length matters. Over 3m may need active cable for 4K 120Hz.
