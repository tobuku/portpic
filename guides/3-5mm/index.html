3.5mm audio. TRS vs TRRS

Count the rings on the plug. That tells you what it carries.

Connector types

  • TRS. Three segments. Tip, Ring, Sleeve. Stereo audio only.
  • TRRS. Four segments. Adds microphone channel. Used by headsets.
  • TS. Two segments. Mono. Used by instruments and older gear.

Compatibility

  • TRS plug in TRRS jack. Audio works. Mic does not.
  • TRRS plug in TRS jack. Audio works. Mic ignored.
  • CTIA vs OMTP. Two TRRS wiring standards. Most modern devices use CTIA.

Common fixes

  • One ear silent. Check cable near plug for damage.
  • Mic not detected. May need TRRS splitter to separate mic and audio.
  • Static noise. Clean the jack with compressed air.
