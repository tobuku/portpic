Optical audio basics

TOSLINK carries digital audio over light. Simple but limited.

What it supports

  • Stereo PCM up to 24-bit 96kHz.
  • Compressed 5.1 surround via Dolby Digital or DTS.
  • Does not support lossless formats like Dolby TrueHD or DTS-HD.

When to use optical

  • Soundbar without HDMI ARC support.
  • Older AV receiver without HDMI inputs.
  • Avoiding ground loop hum from analog connections.

When to switch to HDMI ARC

  • Need lossless audio formats. Use eARC.
  • Want single cable for audio and CEC control.
  • Soundbar supports ARC. Simpler setup.
