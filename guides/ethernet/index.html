Ethernet LED meanings

Two LEDs per port. One for link. One for activity or speed.

LED basics

  • Solid green. Link established. Connection is up.
  • Blinking green. Data activity. Normal traffic.
  • Amber or orange. Often indicates 100Mbps instead of 1Gbps.
  • No light. No link. Check cable, port, or device power.

Cable rules

  • Cat5e supports up to 1Gbps at 100m.
  • Cat6 supports up to 10Gbps at 55m.
  • Cat6a supports 10Gbps at full 100m.
  • Bad crimps cause intermittent drops. Re-terminate or replace.

PoE basics

  • PoE delivers power over Ethernet cable. No separate power adapter.
  • 802.3af provides up to 15.4W per port.
  • 802.3at provides up to 30W per port.
  • Check switch PoE budget before adding devices.
