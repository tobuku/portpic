USB-C. Data, video, power

USB-C is a shape. Features vary by device.

Fast checks

  • Look for a lightning icon. Often signals higher feature support.
  • Look for DisplayPort logo. Often signals video output support.
  • Look for SS marking. Signals higher speed USB modes.

Cable rules

  • Charging works with most USB-C cables, speed differs.
  • Video needs a port with video support and a cable rated for it.
  • High watt charging needs an e-marked cable.

Hub rules

  • A hub cannot add video support if the port lacks it.
  • 4K at higher refresh needs higher bandwidth.
  • Power pass-through needs a charger with enough watt rating.
